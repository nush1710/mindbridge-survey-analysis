# MindBridge: Student Mental Health Survey Analysis

## Project Overview

MindBridge is an academic research project exploring the potential of an AI-powered mental health support chatbot designed for Indian college students.

This project analyses survey responses from 32 college students to understand their mental health support preferences, attitudes towards AI chatbots, adoption barriers and desired features.

## Objectives

- Understand the frequency of college-related stress.
- Explore students' comfort with AI-based mental health support.
- Identify concerns and barriers associated with AI chatbots.
- Analyse preferred chatbot features.
- Translate survey findings into potential product considerations.

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab

## Key Findings

- 75% of respondents experience college-related stress sometimes or very often.
- 50% are comfortable with AI mental health chatbots.
- Incorrect advice was the most frequently reported AI concern (65.62%).
- Stress relief exercises were the most preferred feature (59.38%).
- 87.5% responded that an AI chatbot could be helpful or might be helpful.

## Product Insights

The findings highlight potential opportunities around accessible mental health support, trustworthy AI responses, practical stress-management features and human support integration.

The proposed chatbot is an academic concept; it has not been developed or deployed.

## Limitations

- Small sample size of 32 respondents.
- Self-reported survey data.
- Findings are exploratory and cannot be generalised to all college students.
- Some questions allowed multiple selections.
- Individual-level responses and the original dataset are excluded to protect respondent privacy.

## Repository Contents

- `MindBridge – Student Mental Health Survey Analysis.ipynb` – Jupyter notebook containing the survey analysis, visualisations and product insights.

## Privacy Note

The original survey dataset contains respondent-level information and is not included in this repository. Only aggregate survey results are presented.
